About Me

I am currently working as a postdoctoral fellow at University of Texas Rio Grande Valley. My research interests are diverse and encompass Remote Sensing and GIS, Landscape ecology, Climate Change, and Ecosystem Services.

Outside the lab, I'm also a self-taught cartographer and mixed-media illustrator. My current work at UTRGV is focused on spatial distribution of vector-borne disease.

What I'm Doing

Remote Sensing & GIS

Working with satellite imagery and spatial data to map and monitor landscapes.

Species Distribution Modeling

Modeling species ranges and habitat suitability under environmental change.

Landscape Ecology & Climate Change

Studying protected-area resilience and ecosystem services under a changing climate.

Cartography & Illustration

Making maps and mixed-media artwork as a self-taught cartographer and illustrator.
